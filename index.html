One stack. Same rules for every client.

Boring on purpose. The interesting part should be your brand, not our outages.

Google Cloud, top to bottom

Cloud Run for the apps, Cloud SQL for the data. It scales on a busy Friday and costs little on a Tuesday.

5.2 Sign-in

No passwords for users

Budtenders and customers sign in with a six-digit code by text or email. Nothing to forget, nothing to leak.

5.3 Messaging

Reminders, codes, winner notices and reports go out from the app, on a sender the carriers trust.
